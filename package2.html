Diamond Hotel Philippines

Immerse yourself in the vibrant heart of the Philippines with a luxurious stay at Diamond Hotel Philippines, a 5-star icon overlooking the breathtaking Manila Bay sunset. Located along Roxas Boulevard, this elegant hotel offers world-class comfort, excellent dining, and easy access to the city's best attractions.

This well-paced 10-day package combines relaxing resort days with guided explorations of Manila and top Philippine destinations.

Package Inclusions:

  • Accommodation: 9 nights in a Deluxe Room with Manila Bay views at Diamond Hotel Philippines
  • Daily Breakfast: Buffet breakfast for 2 persons at the hotel
  • Airport Transfers: Private round-trip airport transfers
  • Guided Tours (English-speaking guide + air-conditioned transport):
  • Manila City Tour - Intramuros, Fort Santiago, Rizal Park, Manila Cathedral, and Binondo (Chinatown) food tour
  • Cultural & Heritage Tour - National Museum and Coconut Palace
  • Tagaytay Day Trip - Taal Lake & Volcano viewpoint, picnic lunch, and shopping at Mahogany Market
  • Paganjan Falls Adventure - River rafting and stunning waterfall experience
  • Corregidor Island Day Tour - Historical WWII island tour by ferry

Resort Highlights:

  • Unlimited access to the outdoor swimming pool and fitness center
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the hotel

Special Perks:

  • Welcome drinks & fruit basket upon arrival
  • One romantic sunset dinner at the hotel (for 2)
  • Late check-out (subject to availability)
