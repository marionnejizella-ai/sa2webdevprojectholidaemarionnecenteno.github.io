Jumeirah Marsa Al Arab Hotel

Experience the pinnacle of Arabian luxury at Jumeirah Marsa Al Arab, Dubai's newest ultra-luxury beachfront resprt. Nestled on a private golden peninsula beside the iconic Burj Al Arab, this stunning nautical-inspired retreat offers private beach access, multiple infinity pools, a world-class spa, and breathtaking views of the Arabian Gulf.

This 7-day package perfectly blends relaxation at the resort with guided explorations of Dubai and UAE highlights.

Package Inclusions:

  • Accommodation: 6 nights in a Deluxe Room or Suite with marina or sea views at Jumeirah Marsa Al Arab
  • Daily Breakfast: Sumptous buffet breakfast for 2 persons daily
  • Airport Transfers: Private return airport-hotel transfers
  • Guided Tours (English-speaking professional guide + transportation):
  • Dubai City Tour - Burj Khalifa (At The Top), Dubai Mall, Dubai Fountain Show, Jumeirah Mosque, and Old Dubai (Al Fahidi Historic District)
  • Abu Dhabi Day Trip - Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhai, and Qasr Al Watan (Presidential Palace)
  • Desert Safari Adventure - Dune bashing, camel ride, sandboarding, henna, belly dance & BBQ dinner under the stars
  • Marina & Palm Jumeirah Tour - Photo stops at Burj Al Arab, Atlantis The Palm, and a scenic dhow cruise

Resort Highlights:

  • Unlimited access to private beach & infinity pools
  • Complimentary access to resort facilities and kids/family club (if applicable)

Special Perks:

  • Free high-speed Wi-Fi
  • Late check-out (subject to availability)
